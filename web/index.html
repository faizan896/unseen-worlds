Field guide

  1. Watch the opening. Three probes move for two seconds. The field is sampled at release and stays constant during each run. Probes do not collide.
  2. Change one condition. Each trial resets the chamber and changes probe A only. B and C are untreated references. The dashed path shows A’s opening motion; the crosshair marks its measured endpoint.
  3. Forecast four endpoints. Set the field multiplier for each new release. Zero means coast; −1 means the same field strength in the opposite direction.
  4. Compare notes. The model chooses experiments. A separate predictor averages the endpoints of the catalog rules consistent with the observations.

Coordinates increase east (+x) and south (+y). Mass and charge are dimensionless. Forecast error is the mean distance to the actual endpoints, in chamber units.
