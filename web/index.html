The Unseen Worlds Experiment

Field guide

  1. Watch the opening. Three probes move for two seconds. The field is sampled at release and stays constant during each run. Probes do not collide.
  2. Change one condition. Each trial changes probe A only, then resets to the opening release. B and C are untreated references. The dashed path shows A’s opening motion.
  3. Forecast four endpoints. Each new setup specifies all release conditions. Set the field multiplier relative to the opening acceleration: 0 means coast, −1 means equally strong in the opposite direction.
  4. Compare notes. After submitting, step through the trained policy on the same world. The evaluator—not the network—names the rules eliminated by each measurement.

Coordinates increase east (+x) and south (+y). Mass and charge are dimensionless. Error is mean Euclidean endpoint distance in chamber units; lower is better.
